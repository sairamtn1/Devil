## What is VOLGA OS?

VOLGA OS is a next-generation AI Operating System that unifies:
- **Intelligence**: Brain, Research, Evolution, Self-Modification
- **Execution**: Control Plane, Executor, Orchestrator
- **Creative**: Image Studio, Video Studio
- **Analysis**: World Simulation, Prediction
- **Enterprise**: Organization, Workspace, RBAC, Governance
- **Collaboration**: Collective Intelligence
